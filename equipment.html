Types of Golf Clubs

Driver

Used for long-distance shots from the tee.

Irons

Used for mid-range shots. Includes irons numbered 3 through 9.

Putter

Used on the green to roll the ball into the hole.

Clothing Needed

  • Collared golf shirt
  • Golf shorts or pants
  • Golf shoes
  • Golf glove
  • Hat or visor
