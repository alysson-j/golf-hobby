History of Golf

Golf originated in Scotland during the 15th century. The modern game developed over time, and the first official rules were established in 1744.

Basic Rules

  • 18 holes per round
  • Lowest score wins
  • Each hole has a par
  • Play from tee to green

Benefits

  • Improves fitness
  • Reduces stress
  • Builds focus
  • Encourages social interaction

Fun Facts

The Masters Tournament is played annually at Augusta National Golf Club.

One of the most famous golfers in history is Tiger Woods.
